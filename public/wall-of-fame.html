Science & Innovation

May 12, 2026

Gitanjali Rao

At 11 years old, Gitanjali invented Tethys — a device to detect lead in drinking water — after reading about the Flint water crisis. She went on to create Kindly, an AI-powered app that detects cyberbullying, and was named TIME's Kid of the Year. She now attends MIT. Every adult who doubted her was wrong.
